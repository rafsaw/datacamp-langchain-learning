### Microsoft

**Overview**: Microsoft Corporation is an American multinational corporation and technology conglomerate headquartered in Redmond, Washington. Founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen, Microsoft became influential in the rise of personal computers through its software, particularly Windows. The company has since expanded into various fields, including internet services, cloud computing, and video gaming.

**Key Milestones**:
- **Founding**: Initially developed BASIC interpreters for the Altair 8800.
- **Operating Systems**: Dominated the personal computer operating system market with MS-DOS in the mid-1980s, followed by Windows.
- **IPO**: Went public in 1986, creating significant wealth among its employees.
- **Leadership Changes**: Steve Ballmer became CEO in 2000, followed by Satya Nadella in 2014, who shifted the company's focus towards cloud computing.
- **Acquisitions**: Notable acquisitions include Skype Technologies in 2011 and LinkedIn for $26.2 billion in 2016. In 2023, Microsoft acquired Activision Blizzard for $68.7 billion.

**Products and Services**: Microsoft is known for its Windows operating systems, Microsoft Office suite, Surface hardware, and Xbox gaming consoles. It also provides cloud services through Azure and various consumer internet services.

**Market Position**: Microsoft is one of the Big Five American IT companies and reached a trillion-dollar market cap in 2019.

### Apple Inc.

**Overview**: Apple Inc. is an American multinational corporation and technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple is best known for its consumer electronics, software, and services.

**Key Milestones**:
- **Founding**: Initially focused on producing the Apple I personal computer, followed by the successful Apple II.
- **Innovations**: Introduced the Lisa and Macintosh, some of the first computers with a graphical user interface.
- **Challenges**: Faced significant market share loss in the 1990s and was close to bankruptcy in 1997.
- **Revival**: Jobs returned to Apple after it acquired NeXT, leading to the introduction of iconic products like the iMac, iPod, iPhone, and iPad.

**Products and Services**: Apple's product lineup includes the iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, and Apple TV, along with operating systems like iOS and macOS. It also offers services such as Apple Pay, iCloud, and Apple Music.

**Market Position**: Apple is the largest technology company by revenue, valued at over $3.74 trillion as of December 2024. It was the first publicly traded U.S. company to reach a $1 trillion valuation in 2018.

**Criticism**: Apple has faced criticism regarding labor practices, environmental issues, and anti-competitive behavior, but it maintains a strong brand loyalty among consumers.
